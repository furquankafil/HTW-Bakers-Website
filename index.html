OUR STORY

The sweet side
of Zakir Nagar.

HTW Bakers & Sweets is your neighbourhood bakery on Main Road, Zakir Nagar — serving cakes, pastries, desserts and bakery favourites for the families around Jamia Nagar, Okhla and South Delhi.

Walk in for something small, or order a celebration cake for the whole street. Either way, it leaves our counter fresh.
